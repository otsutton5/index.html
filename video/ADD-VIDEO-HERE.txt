Add your background video to this folder and name it:

hero-background.mp4

Recommended export settings:
- Format: H.264 MP4
- Resolution: 1920 × 1080
- Frame rate: 24, 25 or 30 fps
- No audio needed
- Keep it around 5–15 seconds and loopable
- Aim for under 10 MB where possible

The video must remain muted for autoplay to work reliably.
